How to use Voice Lab

  1. Tap Start recording and allow microphone access.
  2. Say a phrase, then tap Stop.
  3. Choose a fun voice and press Play voice.
  4. Adjust the sliders, or download your changed voice as a WAV file.

Everything happens on this device. Voice Lab does not send recordings to a server.
